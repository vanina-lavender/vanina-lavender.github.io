  • e:koi (github)
    Drexel University, January 2026 - May 2026
    e:koi was made for my CI102 & CI103 courses at Drexel. This is a simulated koi pond using entirely procedural animation for all fish. The simulation includes movement, rudimentary pathfinding, death, and reproduction mechanics.
    Made in Godot with C#.
